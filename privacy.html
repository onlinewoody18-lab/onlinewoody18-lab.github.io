Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

RiftLens is a League of Legends companion app for Windows. This page explains what information it uses and what happens to it. In short: no accounts, no adverts, no tracking, and nothing is sold.

What RiftLens uses

  • Your Riot ID and region, which you type in, so it can look up your ranked games and live game through Riot's official API.
  • Public match data from Riot's API: champions, items, runes and results of ranked games. Statistics such as win rates are worked out from many games together.
  • The League client and game on your PC: champion select and the live game's scoreboard data. These are read locally on your computer.
  • Your own builds, notes and settings, which are saved on your PC.

Where it's stored

In the current version, everything is stored on your own computer, in the RiftLens folder. Nothing is sent anywhere except requests to Riot's API and Data Dragon, the image service Riot runs for champion and item art.

When the public version launches, ranked statistics will come from a shared RiftLens server that collects public match data from Riot's API. That server will receive the Riot ID you look up, so it can fetch that player's games. It won't store anything else about you, and this page will be updated before that version is released.

What RiftLens doesn't do

  • No accounts, passwords or payment details.
  • No adverts, analytics or tracking cookies, in the app or on this website.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No reading of game memory, and no access to anything outside the League client's and game's official local data.

Removing your data

Delete the RiftLens folder on your PC and everything it stored is gone. For anything held on the shared server once it exists, open an issue on GitHub and it will be removed.

Children

RiftLens follows Riot's own age rules for League of Legends, and doesn't knowingly collect anything beyond the public game data described above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
